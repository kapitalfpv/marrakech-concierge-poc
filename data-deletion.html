Data Deletion Instructions

Marrakech Concierge POC

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Marrakech Concierge POC is a proof-of-concept AI-powered hotel concierge service. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the proof of concept.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to:

kapitalfpv@gmail.com

Use the subject line:

Marrakech Concierge POC - Data Deletion Request

Please provide enough information for us to identify the data associated with your use of the service. For example, you may provide the WhatsApp telephone number or identifier used to communicate with the concierge.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, financial information, passport details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in your request.

What Happens Next

We may contact you if additional information is reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records or verify the request.

Once the relevant information has been identified, we will delete or anonymise personal information that we are responsible for retaining, subject to any information that must be retained for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate compliance purposes.

Third-Party Services

The proof of concept may use third-party services such as the WhatsApp Business Platform, hosting infrastructure, and artificial intelligence services. Information processed independently by those providers may also be subject to their own privacy and deletion procedures.
