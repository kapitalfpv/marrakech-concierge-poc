Privacy Policy

Marrakech Concierge POC

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Marrakech Concierge POC is a proof-of-concept AI-powered hotel concierge service designed to explore guest assistance through messaging channels such as WhatsApp.

Information We Process

When you communicate with the Marrakech Concierge POC, we may process information necessary to provide and test the service, including:

  • Messages you send to the concierge;
  • Your WhatsApp identifier or telephone number as supplied through the WhatsApp platform;
  • Basic messaging metadata such as message identifiers and timestamps;
  • Information you voluntarily provide during a conversation, such as language or travel preferences.

How We Use Information

Information is processed for purposes such as:

  • Responding to concierge requests;
  • Testing and improving the proof-of-concept service;
  • Troubleshooting technical problems;
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse.

AI Processing

Messages may be processed using artificial intelligence services in order to generate concierge responses. Information is only sent to service providers where necessary to operate or test the service.

WhatsApp and Meta

The proof of concept may use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Messages and related information may therefore also be processed by Meta according to the terms and privacy policies applicable to its services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by technology providers that are necessary to operate the proof of concept, including messaging, hosting, and artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for testing, operation, security, troubleshooting, and applicable legal requirements. Because this is a proof of concept, retention practices may change as the service develops.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Marrakech Concierge POC.

Instructions for submitting a deletion request are available on our Data Deletion page.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information processed by the proof of concept. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the proof of concept develops. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
